Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 13, 2026

1. What Chawso PDF is

Chawso PDF is a set of PDF tools based on the open-source BentoPDF project. Its tools are built to run in your web browser, so that the documents you work on stay on your own device.

2. Local processing

The tools on this site process your files locally in your browser using JavaScript and WebAssembly. Your documents are not uploaded to our servers to be processed, and processed copies are saved by your browser directly to your device. Because processing happens on your device, we do not see the content of your files.

3. Downloaded program components

Some tools download additional program code or data the first time you use them: for example the OCR (text recognition) engine and its language data, and certain document-conversion engines. These downloads come from third-party content-delivery networks and contain program code and language data, not your documents. The processing they enable still runs on your device. Your files themselves are not sent to these services.

4. Information we do not collect

Chawso PDF does not require an account. This site does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking scripts, and we do not collect:

  • The content of the files you open with the tools.
  • Personal data contained within your documents.
  • The filenames of your documents.
  • Usage profiles or behavioural metrics about your session.

5. Third-party open-source software

Chawso PDF is built on open-source libraries, including BentoPDF, PDF.js, and pdf-lib. See our licensing page for the full list of components and their licences.

6. Security

Because your files are processed on your own device and are not transmitted to our servers for processing, they are not exposed to server-side storage or transit risks on our side. The security of your documents also depends on the security of your own device and browser.

7. Children's privacy

This service is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes are posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the contact address published on the main Chawso website.
