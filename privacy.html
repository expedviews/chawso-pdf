Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 13, 2026

1. What Chawso PDF is

Chawso PDF is a set of PDF tools based on the open-source Chawso PDF project. Its tools are designed to run in your web browser, with the aim that the documents you work on remain on your own device rather than being sent to a server for processing.

2. Local processing by design

Chawso PDF is designed so that the tools on this site process your files locally in your browser using JavaScript and WebAssembly. The tools are not built to upload your documents to our servers for processing, and processed copies are saved by your browser directly to your device. Because processing is designed to happen on your device, the service is not intended to see the content of your files.

3. Downloaded program components

Some tools download additional program code or data the first time you use them: for example the OCR (text recognition) engine and its language data, and certain document-conversion engines. These downloads come from third-party content-delivery networks and contain program code and language data, not your documents. The processing they enable still runs on your device. In this design these services act as download sources only; the tools are not built to send your files to them.

4. Information the service is not designed to collect

Chawso PDF does not require an account, and the site is designed to operate without analytics, advertising, or tracking scripts. The service is not designed to collect:

  • The content of the files you open with the tools.
  • Personal data contained within your documents.
  • The filenames of your documents.
  • Usage profiles or behavioural metrics about your session.

Like any website, the hosting and content-delivery providers that serve these pages (and the third-party CDNs that supply some program components) receive standard connection metadata as part of delivering the site, such as your IP address and the time and URL of each request. Their handling of that connection data is covered by their own privacy policies.

5. Third-party open-source software

Chawso PDF is built on open-source libraries, including Chawso PDF, PDF.js, and pdf-lib. See our licensing page for the list of bundled components and their licences.

6. Security

Because the tools are designed to process your files on your own device rather than transmit them to our servers for processing, the service is not intended to expose them to server-side storage or transit risks on our side. The security of your documents also depends on the security of your own device and browser.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes are posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
